# Patañjali Yoga Sūtras Telugu Translation As It Is. 

The purpose of this project is to present the original Sanskrit sūtras along with their Telugu translations, while preserving the meaning and terminology of the original Sanskrit text as accurately as possible.

I am not a scholar or professor of Sanskrit or Telugu literature. I have not studied the Vedas or higher texts under a guru or formal guidance. I am simply a practitioner, studying the Yoga texts on my own.

Along the way, I have translated these sutras with the help of AI and wanted to share them here, in the hope that they may be useful to someone who is sincerely trying to understand and discover the truth for themselves.

My sincere apologies for any mistakes or inaccuracies in the sutras, translations, or explanations. I welcome corrections and suggestions from those who have deeper knowledge and understanding of these texts.

## Contents

* Samādhi Pāda
* Sādhana Pāda
* Vibhūti Pāda
* Kaivalya Pāda

## Translation Approach

The translation focuses on:

* The original Sanskrit sūtra
* Accurate representation of Sanskrit terminology
* Clear and natural Telugu without adding concepts that are not present in the original sūtra

The primary purpose is translation rather than commentary or interpretation.
