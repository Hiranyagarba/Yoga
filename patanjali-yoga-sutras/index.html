A PERSONAL STUDY

Shared with sincerity.

I am not a scholar or professor of Sanskrit or Telugu literature. I have not studied the Vedas or higher texts under a guru or formal guidance. I am simply a practitioner, studying the Yoga texts on my own.

Along the way, I have translated these sūtras with the help of AI and wanted to share them here, in the hope that they may be useful to someone who is sincerely trying to understand and discover the truth for themselves.

My sincere apologies for any mistakes or inaccuracies in the sūtras, translations, or explanations. Corrections and suggestions are welcome.
