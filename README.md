# Yoga

A study and practice of Yoga based on the classical texts and traditions of India.

This project explores Yoga through the original Sanskrit sources, their meanings, commentaries, and practical application.

## Texts

* Patañjali Yoga Sūtras
* Sāṃkhya Kārikā
* Bhagavad Gītā
* Haṭha Yoga Pradīpikā
* Vijñāna Bhairava Tantra
* Other traditional Yoga texts

## Approach

The aim is to study these texts carefully, understand their original meaning, and bring their teachings into practical life without losing their traditional context.

## Project

This repository contains research, translations, explanations, and practical studies related to Yoga.
